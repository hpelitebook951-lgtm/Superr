Methodology & data notes

A transparent summary of the supplied research package. See the included audit reports for full reproduction details.

Source audit · 2026
Scoring and selection

Batters

Recommended batting list combines 60% long-term pressure rating with 40% separate recent-form score. Long-term delivery weights use a two-year half-life; recent form uses the player's last ten appearances in the training window.

Bowlers

Primary rating retains long-term pressure score. The last-ten-appearance score appears separately and is not included in the rating.

Pressure context

Pressure is an operational index based on innings phase, first-innings stress or chase required run rate and wickets lost. Historical baselines are fitted before each temporal cutoff and frozen for the following evaluation period.

Consistency and evidence volume

Confidence bands use pressure exposure and pressure-match counts. The scorecard Wilson interval describes uncertainty in the match-level consistency proportion only; it is not a rating interval.

Role labels & caveats

Batting role

Inferred from median batting position in supplied playing-XI records: 1–2 opener, 3–5 top/middle order, 6+ finisher/lower order. Descriptive only; not used to alter rating.

Bowling role

Inferred from recorded pace/spin delivery mix; 70% or more is labeled Pace or Spin, otherwise Mixed/varied. A usage proxy, not a fixed identity.

Dataset scope

Supplied data covers IPL and T20I through 1 November 2026. It is a static research snapshot; there is no live feed and no claim that the sample is specific to Rajasthan Royals' current squad.

Known data-quality checks

853 in-scope match rows; 16 have no delivery records. 23 revised-target matches are flagged for manual scorecard/provider confirmation. `other` extras are provisionally treated as illegal; one seven-delivery-over anomaly requires inspection.

Do not describe revised-target handling as fully DLS validated. A revised target alone does not establish interruption timing, method, par score or resource calculation.
